# Decisions required from the club

No item below blocks the preliminary discovery work. Items marked **before
prototype** affect Phase 2; the remainder must be settled before migration or
launch.

## Governance and ownership

1. **Before prototype:** Who is the project owner with final acceptance
   authority?
2. **Before prototype:** Who owns content approval for each sport/activity and
   for club-wide legal, membership and rental information?
3. Which GitHub organization/account will own the repository and Pages site?
4. Which people require technical administrator access, and which should be
   email-invited Pages CMS collaborators only?
5. What is the minimum two-person succession/backup arrangement for the
   repository, domain and CMS?

## Scope and information architecture

6. **Before prototype:** Are Lauftreff and Kindersport formal departments,
   activities, or another organizational type?
7. **Before prototype:** Is Tübingen United presented as an SSC department or a
   cooperation with SSC and SV Bühl?
8. Should American Football remain primarily a gateway to `red-knights.de`, or
   should material also be maintained on the SSC site?
9. Which historic news and team archives must remain public?
10. Should `Sportangebote` be the public navigation label, or does the club
    prefer `Abteilungen`?

## Content and privacy

11. Which current names, direct email addresses, telephone numbers and team
    photographs are approved for republication?
12. What publication/consent process applies to photographs of children and
    teams?
13. Are public bank account details necessary on web pages and downloadable
    forms, or should donation/payment instructions use a narrower route?
14. Which sponsor logos and photographs have confirmed web-use rights?
15. Who will review the legal notice and privacy statement for the final
    services and integrations?

## Documents and operational facts

16. Which membership application, statutes, privacy rules and contribution
    rules are the currently approved versions?
17. Which clubhouse rental contract and prices are current? Search-indexed
    files show conflicting versions.
18. Which training times, venues, team rosters and contacts are current for the
    next publication date?
19. Should obsolete public documents remain available for historical/legal
    reasons, or be removed after redirects/notice pages are established?

## Features and integrations

20. Is a contact form genuinely required, or are role-based mail links
    sufficient?
21. Is an event calendar needed for launch, and who will maintain it?
22. Are simple external links acceptable for league tables/results, or are
    embedded displays required?
23. Is on-site search necessary at launch given the expected content volume?
24. Are visitor analytics required? The recommendation is none by default.

## Infrastructure and launch

25. Who controls the current domain, DNS, hosting and email service?
26. Who can supply a DNS zone export and confirm MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records
    before launch planning?
27. Can the current host repair the invalid TLS certificate or provide a safe
    read-only public-site export for discovery?
28. What rollback period must the old hosting remain active after launch?
